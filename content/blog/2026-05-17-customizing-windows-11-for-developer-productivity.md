As developers, we spend most of our time staring at our screens, typing away at our keyboards. Our workflow is heavily dependent on the tools and environment we use. Recently, Microsoft announced that it's testing an update for Windows 11 that will allow users to change the position of the taskbar and resize the Start button. This might seem like a minor update, but for developers, it can be a game-changer. 
Imagine being able to customize your taskbar to fit your specific needs, having all the tools and applications you need at your fingertips, without having to navigate through multiple menus. This level of customization can significantly impact our productivity and focus. 
## What Actually Happened
The update, which is currently being tested, allows users to move the taskbar to the top, bottom, or either side of the screen. Additionally, the Start button can be resized to fit the user's needs. This level of customization is a significant departure from the current version of Windows 11, where the taskbar is fixed at the bottom of the screen and the Start button is a fixed size. 
To give you a better idea of what this update entails, here are some of the key features:
* Adjustable taskbar position: users can move the taskbar to the top, bottom, or either side of the screen
* Resizable Start button: users can resize the Start button to fit their needs
* Improved workflow: with the ability to customize the taskbar and Start button, users can create a workflow that is tailored to their specific needs
## Why Developers Should Care
As developers, we are always looking for ways to improve our productivity and focus. This update can help us do just that. By allowing us to customize our workflow, we can reduce distractions and stay focused on the task at hand. 
For example, if you're working on a project that requires you to have multiple applications open at the same time, you can move the taskbar to the side of the screen to free up space. Or, if you prefer to have your Start button in a specific location, you can resize it to fit your needs.

From a technical standpoint, this update is significant because it shows that Microsoft is committed to listening to user feedback and making changes to improve the overall user experience. 
The update is currently being tested, and it's expected to be rolled out to all Windows 11 users in the near future.

2. Roll out the update to a larger group of users, including developers and power users
