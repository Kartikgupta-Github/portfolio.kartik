From a technical perspective, the decision to build cheaper in-house models is likely driven by the need to reduce computational costs and increase efficiency. By building its own in-house models, Microsoft can optimize these models for its specific use cases and reduce the computational overhead. For example, Microsoft could use techniques such as:
